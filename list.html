Ingredients That Makes Up Rice and Beans With Ayamase Stew

  • Rice
  • Beans
  • Pepper & Onions
  • Palm oil & groundnut oil
  • Locust beans
  • Seasoning Cubes & Salt
  • Eggs
  • Assorted eat

How to prepare rice and beans

The first Step:
Wash your pot
The second step:
Put on your gas
The third step:
Place your washed pot on fire and pour water inside your pot
The fourth step:
Rinse your beans into the pot of water you placed on the gas
The fifth step:
Wait for about 30-35 mins before adding your thoroughly rinsed rice into the pot of beans on fire
The sixth step:
Add salt to taste into your rice and beans on fire and you can add few drops of groundnut oil
The seventh step:
Once the food is ready (based on preference),you can put your pot of rice and beans aside and start to make preparation for your stew

How to make ayamase stew

The first step:
Rinse your pot
The second step:
Put on your gas
The third step:
Place your washed pot on the gas and pour your palm oil and groundnut oil in equal proportion into the pot placed on the fire
The fourth step:
Bleach the palm oil by leaving it for long on the fire and you know if it is bleached when the colour has changed Also Note: that if left on the fire for too long,it might turn to fire which can be dangerous
The fifth step:
Inside your pot of bleached palm oil,pour in your washed locust beans and immediately add your chopped onions,stir till your onions is almost brown
The sixth step:
Inside the pot of bleached palm oil that has your has your locust beans and onions,add your not smoothly blended pepper
The seventh step:
Add your stock next,put your boiled eggs,your fried assorted meat into the pepper mix you put on the fire,wait for about 3 mins before tasting and putting the necessary seasoning cubes and salt to taste
The final step:
Wait till the stew is well fried before taking it off the fire,and once it is well fried,you have your delicious ayamase stew ready to be eaten with your well made rice and beans.
